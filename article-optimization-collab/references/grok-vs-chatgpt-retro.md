# Grok vs ChatGPT Article Retro

Use this as the seed memory for future article optimizations in the user's `文章优化` project.

## What Worked

- The user prefers a flexible optimization workflow, not a rigid article brief.
- The fastest collaboration pattern is: diagnose current article, give a modification map, then write copy-ready replacements.
- `Ctrl+F` anchors are better than line numbers for manual WordPress edits.
- Hands-on tests should show actual task performance differences between models, not just ask models for claims.
- Screenshots should be planned as part of the reader experience: where tested, what prompt, what image, where inserted, and alt text.
- Official screenshots and benchmark cards are supporting evidence, not substitutes for hands-on tests.
- GlobalGPT mentions work best when tied to multi-model comparison, daily use, affordability, and reduced switching.

## Friction to Avoid

- Do not confuse FAQ content with editor notes.
- Do not place screenshots after unrelated short answers just because an image exists.
- Do not use vague proof language such as "this screenshot is important".
- Do not expose backend SEO language such as URL labels, redirect strategy, proof-image labels, or article consolidation notes in public copy.
- Do not give line numbers for locating edits.
- Do not overuse benchmark caveats; state the data directly and explain the practical takeaway.
- Do not treat GlobalGPT as a replacement for official developer products.

## User Language Preferences

- Use `in the test` or `in the picture`, not `in the screenshot`.
- Avoid `currently shows`, `benchmark data points in the same direction`, and other stiff AI phrases.
- Use concrete observed differences and practical recommendations.
- Keep product promotion natural and moderate.

## Reusable Review Checklist

- Does the article sound like a real reviewer tested the tools?
- Are public paragraphs free of editor/backend notes?
- Are real tests connected to the screenshots?
- Are benchmark or official-doc claims direct and sourced?
- Are tables readable in WordPress?
- Are internal links natural?
- Are image filenames, alt text, and insertion notes separate from body copy?
- Are dynamic prices and model names verified?
